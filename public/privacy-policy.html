The Final Check

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  |  Applies to: The Final Check mobile app and web portal

In plain English: This app is a professional tool used by hospitality consultants. We collect the minimum data needed to run the service, we don't sell it to anyone, and we store it securely in the EU.

1. Who we are

The Final Check is a hospitality consultancy management platform operated by Jason Wardill trading as The Final Check, based in the United Kingdom.

For data protection purposes, Jason Wardill is the data controller for all personal data processed through this application.

Contact us at: info@thefinalcheck.uk

2. What data we collect and why

Account data

  • Email address and password (for authentication)
  • Your name (displayed in the app)

We collect this to let you log in and use the platform.

Client and business data

  • Names, addresses, and contact details of hospitality businesses you manage
  • Business information such as turnover, headcount, and operating category

We collect this because it is the core purpose of the app — managing your client portfolio.

Audit and assessment data

  • Results of kitchen audits, food safety inspections, and mystery shopper visits
  • Scores, observations, photographs, and consultant notes

We store audit records so you can track progress over time and generate reports for clients.

Questionnaire responses

  • Pre-visit questionnaire answers submitted by your clients via a shared link

These are collected on behalf of the consultant to prepare for site visits.

Usage and technical data

  • Device type and operating system (collected by the app platform)
  • App preferences you set (e.g. default landing page, colour scheme)

We use this to provide a consistent experience across sessions and devices.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal grounds under UK GDPR:

  • Contract performance — to provide you with the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — to improve the platform and ensure its security
  • Consent — where you have explicitly provided data via intake forms or questionnaires

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the following service providers who help us run the platform:

  • Supabase (database and authentication) — data is stored on AWS infrastructure, primarily in EU regions. Supabase is GDPR-compliant and ISO 27001 certified. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Vercel (web hosting) — the web app is served via Vercel's global CDN. See vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account is active
  • Audit and client records are retained for as long as you use the service
  • If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days

6. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are never stored in plain text — authentication is handled by Supabase using industry-standard hashing. Access to the platform requires a valid account; client data is not publicly accessible.

7. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data while a dispute is resolved

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@thefinalcheck.uk. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

8. Cookies and local storage

The app uses browser local storage to save your preferences (such as your default landing page and display settings). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics scripts.

9. Children

This platform is a professional business tool. It is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
